The license key is in the receipt email from Polar and in your customer portal. Activating it takes under a minute.

  1. Copy the key. It looks like BULLPANE-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Receipt email, or your Polar customer portal.
  2. Open your Bullpane, Settings → License. You need the admin account.
  3. Paste and click Activate. Alerts, Users & roles, Folders, Flows and the Audit log unlock immediately. No restart.

Running from Docker without a UI handy? Set BULLPANE_LICENSE_KEY in the environment instead; it activates a few seconds after boot.
One key runs one installation. Moving servers: remove the key on the old one first. Anything odd: hello@bullpane.com.
