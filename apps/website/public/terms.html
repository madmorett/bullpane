4. How license checking works

A Pro installation contacts api.bullpane.com to activate the key and then about once a day to confirm it is still valid. It sends only the key, an activation identifier and a label for the installation (its hostname and public URL) plus the Bullpane version. It never sends your queues, your jobs, your job data, your Redis addresses or your credentials. If our server cannot be reached, the installation keeps working with Pro features for seven days and then falls back to the free edition. Nothing is deleted.

Air-gapped or offline installations can be issued a key that never contacts us. Write to hello@bullpane.com.
